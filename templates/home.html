How It Works

Three simple steps from complaint to resolution

📍

Report an Issue

Pin the exact location on the map, describe the problem and attach a photo. Takes under a minute.

📊

Track in Real Time

Watch your complaint move through Pending → In Progress → Resolved on a live Kanban board.

🔔

Get Notified

Receive instant notifications whenever the status of your complaint changes.
